\subsection{Activity Goal}
The primary objective of this laboratory session is the design, implementation, and experimental validation of advanced state-space control strategies for the angular position of a DC servomotor. The activity is structured into two main parts: first, the enhancement of a pre-existing PID controller through the integration of an anti-windup scheme and a multi-term feedforward compensator for friction and inertia. Second, the development of a continuous-time position control system using state-space techniques, addressing both nominal tracking requirements and robust tracking in the presence of disturbances and time-varying references.
