\subsubsection{Nominal Tracking Design}
The state-space controller was designed based on the pole-placement technique. The closed-loop eigenvalues were determined using a second-order dominant pole approximation to satisfy the specifications of $t_{s,5\%} \le 0.15$ s and $M_p \le 10\%$. Nominal steady-state tracking of constant references was achieved by calculating the feedforward gains $N_x$ and $N_u$.
